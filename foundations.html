Foundation types

Choose a foundation system around soil conditions, building weight, budget, and long-term maintenance expectations.

Universal

Monolithic slab

A reinforced concrete plate that distributes loads evenly and works well for energy-efficient homes.

Classic

Strip foundation

A cost-effective option for predictable soils and buildings with clear load-bearing wall lines.

Complex ground

Pile foundation

Piles transfer loads to deeper, more stable layers when upper soils are weak or inconsistent.
